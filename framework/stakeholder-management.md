# Stakeholder Management Framework

## Stakeholder Mapping

For each stakeholder, clarify:

| Dimension | Questions |
|---|---|
| Role | What is the stakeholder responsible for? |
| Interest | What outcome matters to them? |
| Influence | What decisions can they affect? |
| Dependency | What does the project need from them? |
| Communication | What information and cadence are required? |
| Escalation | When and how should blockers be raised? |

## Coordination Principle

In cross-functional environments without direct authority, effective delivery depends on:

**Alignment + Transparency + Follow-up + Accountability**

## Practical Coordination Loop

**Identify → Align → Assign → Follow Up → Escalate → Close**
