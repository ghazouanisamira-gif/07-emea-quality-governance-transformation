# 08 — Results & Lessons Learned

## Documented Outcomes

| Indicator | Result |
|---|---:|
| Quality Score | **40% → 85%** |
| Errors | **−20%** |
| CSAT | **+15 pts** |
| Onboarding | **+40%** |
| Inter-rater consistency | **>90%** |

## What the Results Demonstrate

The results reflect the combined effect of:

- stronger quality governance;
- structured feedback;
- targeted training;
- root-cause analysis;
- cross-functional coordination;
- performance monitoring;
- continuous improvement;
- better use of digital tools.

The indicators should therefore be understood as outcomes of an integrated operating model rather than isolated actions.

## Key Lessons

### Governance

Complex multi-provider environments require explicit governance mechanisms, especially when direct hierarchical authority is limited.

### Quality

Quality control becomes more valuable when it is connected to learning, corrective action and operational management.

### Data

Performance data needs context and interpretation before it can support sound management decisions.

### Technology

Successful tool deployment requires continuous coordination between operational users, business stakeholders and technical teams.

### Transformation

Sustainable improvement depends on closing the loop:

**Measure → Understand → Act → Verify → Sustain**
