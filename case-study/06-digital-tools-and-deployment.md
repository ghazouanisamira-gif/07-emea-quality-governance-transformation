# 06 — Digital Tools & Deployment

## Tool Ecosystem

The mandate involved several operational and analytical tools, including:

- EvaluAgent
- Diabolocom
- Zendesk
- Tableau
- Qlik

## Contribution to Tool Selection

The role included contribution to comparative analysis of solutions and to the evaluation / selection process, including EvaluAgent.

The focus was on operational fit, quality requirements, reporting needs and usability for the teams.

## Business–IT Interface

The role acted as a key interface between operational / quality stakeholders and IT.

Responsibilities included:

- translating business needs into actionable requirements;
- clarifying quality and reporting expectations;
- coordinating testing;
- identifying functional issues;
- reporting data inconsistencies;
- validating corrections;
- communicating operational feedback;
- supporting tool evolution.

## Agile Testing & Deployment

During critical deployment phases, the operating rhythm was iterative:

**Test → Identify → Document → Report → Correct → Retest**

Frequent follow-up points helped accelerate identification of:

- functional gaps;
- data inconsistencies;
- reporting issues;
- user needs;
- process / tool misalignment.

## Qlik Reporting Evolution

Tracking sheets and reporting structures evolved based on quality and operational feedback.

The objective was not simply to deploy a tool, but to ensure that the resulting information remained useful for operational monitoring and management decisions.

## Key Learning

Digital transformation in operations requires continuous coordination between:

**Business Need + Process + Users + Data + Technology**

The contribution was therefore on the **business / operational side of digital deployment**, not software development.
