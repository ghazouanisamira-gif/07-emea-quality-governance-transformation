# 06 — Digital Tools & Deployment

## Tool Ecosystem

The mandate involved several operational and analytical tools, including:

- EvaluAgent
- Diabolocom
- Zendesk
- Tableau
- Qlik

## Contribution to Tool Selection

The role included contributing to comparative analysis of available solutions and supporting the selection process, including the evaluation of EvaluAgent.

## Deployment Coordination

During implementation and testing phases, coordination included:

- frequent cross-functional follow-up;
- issue identification and escalation;
- detection of data inconsistencies;
- validation of operational needs;
- feedback to technical stakeholders;
- improvement requests for reporting and dashboards.

## Qlik Reporting Evolution

Operational and quality feedback was used to improve Qlik tracking and make reporting more aligned with business and quality needs.

## Agile Way of Working

The deployment phase used an iterative approach characterized by:

**Test → Identify → Feedback → Correct → Retest**

This supported faster identification of functional gaps and data-quality issues.

## Key Learning

Digital transformation was treated as a joint **business + operational + technology** effort rather than a purely technical implementation.
