# 05 — Training & Capability Development

## Quality-to-Training Loop

The mandate connected quality monitoring directly with capability development.

**Quality Finding → Root Cause → Targeted Training → Application → Follow-up**

## Key Initiatives

- Corrective training modules
- Multilingual e-learning
- Gamified learning activities
- Quizzes and rankings
- Training library
- Feedback linked to quality results
- CSAT analysis by agent and platform

## E-Learning Development

Two e-learning modules were designed around the digital quality environment:

1. A multilingual, gamified training library.
2. A personalized CSAT analysis dashboard by agent and platform.

The approach helped move training from generic delivery toward **data-informed and targeted capability development**.

## People Development Perspective

The model combined:

- Quality coaching
- Corrective learning
- Performance feedback
- Continuous follow-up
- Capability building
