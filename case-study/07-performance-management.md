# 07 — Performance Management

## Performance Dimensions

The framework combined several dimensions:

- Quality Score
- CSAT
- Error / non-conformity monitoring
- Onboarding
- Inter-rater consistency
- Market / provider performance
- Corrective action progress

## Management Cycle

**Measure → Analyze → Challenge → Act → Monitor**

Performance data was used to:

- identify gaps;
- investigate root causes;
- prioritize corrective actions;
- support targeted training;
- monitor improvement;
- inform management discussions.

## Beyond Reporting

The objective was not simply to produce dashboards.

Performance management connected **data → interpretation → action → follow-up**.

This made quality reporting an operational management tool and supported continuous improvement across the EMEA ecosystem.
