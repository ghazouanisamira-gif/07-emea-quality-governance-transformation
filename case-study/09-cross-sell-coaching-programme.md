# 09 — Cross-Sell Coaching Programme

## Overview

Within the EMEA mandate, a **6-month, performance-oriented coaching programme** was designed and deployed, as a **first improvement exercise on business value (BV) and average basket for the B2B Presales activity** of an international e-commerce client, with the objective of increasing cross-selling. The team grew significantly as the operation scaled.

Cross-selling then became an **integrated, ongoing routine**, worked on continuously as production objectives evolve; the six months described here are the first measured phase.

## Scope

The first exercise targeted **B2B Presales**. It started with a small team and grew gradually as the operation scaled, with additional agents available as B2C reinforcement during peak flows.

It was subsequently **extended to B2C to increase the average basket**. The channel mix differed by activity: **B2C was 100% inbound**, while **B2B was about 80% outbound and 20% inbound**. After the move to One Presales, agents became **multi-skilled** and handled outbound, inbound, email and chat.

The operational evolution of the mandate itself is described in [02 — Governance Model](02-governance-model.md).

## Contribution

Design and deployment of the coaching system: sales-performance focus, coaching routines, follow-up with operations managers and performance tracking.

## Approach

The programme was part of the quality and training plan and relied on the following mechanisms:

- **Commercial approach:** training aimed at switching a call from customer service to a sales opportunity, delivered through role-play sessions ("sketchings") built on the top call reasons and real customer cases, for all customer types.
- **End-to-end process:** role-plays followed the full path, from logging information in the CRM to the follow-up email sent after the conversation.
- **Joint calibration:** role-plays and debriefs run together with the client's sales agents and the quality analysts, with calibration meetings to align evaluation expectations.
- **Tooling:** quizzes, call listening and evaluations in the quality platform; a dedicated cross-sell tool covered in the training plan.
- **Delivery model:** a four-step deployment process (request, preparation, organization, delivery and follow-up), with operational relays (managers, coaches, team leaders and quality analysts) acting as training relays for agents.

## Documented Outcomes

| Indicator | Before | After | Change |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Products per basket | **1.7** | **3** | **+76%** |
| Average basket | **€250** | **€350** | **+40%** |

### Indicator definitions

- **Products per basket:** average number of products per order.
- **Average basket:** average order value.
- Programme duration: 6 months. Population: a team scaled progressively during the programme.

## Limits

- Results reflect the combined effect of coaching, operational follow-up and the wider quality-governance model; they should not be attributed to a single action.
- Detailed datasets, individual agent performance and client-identifying information are excluded.
