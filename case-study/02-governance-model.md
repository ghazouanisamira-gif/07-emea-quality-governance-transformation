# 02 — Governance Model

## Governance Objective

Create a common governance structure capable of coordinating multiple providers, markets and internal stakeholders while maintaining clear accountability and performance visibility.

## Governance Components

- Common quality framework
- Defined roles and responsibilities
- Performance indicators
- Reporting and review routines
- Action-plan follow-up
- Issue escalation
- Cross-functional coordination
- Continuous-improvement feedback loops

## Operating Environment

The model covered:

- 5 EMEA markets
- External service providers
- In-house teams
- Quality and training functions
- Operations and management
- Technology / IT stakeholders
- Other project and business stakeholders

## Leadership Model

A key characteristic was **coordination without direct hierarchical authority**.

The role therefore relied on:

- stakeholder alignment;
- clear communication;
- structured follow-up;
- evidence-based recommendations;
- escalation when required;
- collaborative problem solving.

## Team Scope

The mandate operated with a dedicated team of **12 collaborators**, dedicated management and an allocated budget.
