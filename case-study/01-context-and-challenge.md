# 01 — Context & Challenge

## Context

A major international e-commerce client operated across several European markets and service environments.

Between **2020 and 2023**, the mandate focused on building and coordinating a common quality and governance approach across:

- France
- Italy
- Spain
- Germany
- United Kingdom

The ecosystem included external service providers and in-house teams.

## Core Challenge

The challenge was not simply to monitor quality. It was to establish a sustainable operating model capable of:

- harmonizing quality practices;
- coordinating multiple stakeholders;
- creating consistent performance standards;
- connecting quality findings to corrective training;
- improving customer satisfaction;
- supporting digital tool deployment;
- creating reliable feedback loops for continuous improvement.

The mandate operated without direct hierarchical authority over all participating actors, making stakeholder alignment and governance central to delivery.

## Transformation Logic

**Structure → Govern → Coordinate → Deploy → Measure → Improve**

This approach combined quality management, project coordination, stakeholder management, performance monitoring and continuous improvement.
