# 04 — Quality Framework

## Multichannel Quality Management

The quality framework covered:

- Voice
- Email
- Chat

## Core Mechanisms

### Quality Monitoring

Structured evaluation of customer interactions across participating environments.

### Real-Time Feedback

Quality findings were fed back to operational teams to shorten the distance between observation and corrective action.

### Critical Situations

Specific handling of unacceptable / critical situations was integrated into the quality approach.

### Calibration

Calibration mechanisms supported evaluator alignment and improved consistency between quality assessments.

### Root-Cause Analysis

Quality and customer-satisfaction gaps were analyzed to identify underlying causes rather than only reporting symptoms.

### Corrective Actions

Findings were translated into targeted improvement and training actions.

## Quality Improvement Logic

**Evaluate → Feedback → Analyze → Correct → Verify → Improve**
